Artwork scope — Elephant Edition 6.0

The sixteen lion frames and standalone capture-brush.webp are unchanged from
the approved Painted Edition. A new thirteen-pose elephant sequence and portrait
have been added. ART_ELEPHANT.txt documents the selected source frames.
Full elephant/tiger scenes are only used for that pairing. Other elephant
targets retain the correct defender through split-panel framing.
Six other species retain their existing painted cut-ins, not new pose films.
See README.txt for resolution and animation limits; CREDITS.txt for provenance.
