# Approved lion–tiger full animation

Scale-continuity update: see [lion-tiger-scale-fix.md](lion-tiger-scale-fix.md). The current landing shot uses a corrected atlas without storyboard-image swaps. The assembly below records the original v3 build.

User approved the four keyframes and requested the full animation. Generated with the built-in image tool, using `docs/artwork/lion-tiger-action-study-v3.png` as the reference for every shot. Original approved keyframe pixels are also retained in the film at the leap, first contact, knockback and final landing.

## Final assembly

- Runtime assets: `game/assets/lion-tiger-leap-v3.webp`, `lion-vs-tiger-sheet-contact.webp`, `lion-tiger-fall-v3.webp`, and `lion-vs-tiger-sheet-key-poses.webp`.
- Three new atlases each contain 16 drawings at 1536 × 1024. They are encoded as WebP at quality 94. The approved storyboard is encoded losslessly; its four panels are cropped at runtime with a 2-pixel inset to exclude panel borders.
- The displayed sequence has 44 poses: 16 leap poses, 12 contact poses, and 16 knockback/landing poses. The first four generated close-up approach cells are omitted to cut directly onto the approved impact. This also avoids showing the generated shoulder scratches before contact.
- Approved keyframe pixels replace displayed poses 16, 17, 29 and 44 (one-based). All remaining poses come from the new atlases.
- Action advances at 16 drawings per second, with a 0.22-second opening hold, 0.16-second impact hold, and 0.70-second final hold. Contact sound is aligned with pose 17; the ground thud is aligned with pose 34. A bounded impact jolt is disabled in reduced motion. Calligraphy appears after knockback, leaving the close collision unobscured.
- Reduced motion uses three static poses. Missing art falls back to the existing species-correct lion/tiger panels. Normal completion and skipping retain one capture commitment.
- The original lion/leopard and elephant/tiger media, timing and rendering are preserved.
